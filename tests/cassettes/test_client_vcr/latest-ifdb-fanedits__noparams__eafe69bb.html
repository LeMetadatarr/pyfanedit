Original Movie/Show Title:
  • Severance (2022)
Fanedit Type:
TV-to-Movie
Fanedit Release Date:
April 2026
Fanedit Running Time:
246 minutes
Subtitles Available:
No
Synopsis:
This is a fan edit of the first season of the fantastic show Severance, which focuses solely on the perspectives of the "Innies." All perspectives from non-severed characters and outie personas have been removed, allowing the viewer to follow along with the story more in line with the Innie's experience. The main goal of this edit was to allow the viewer to have the same lack-of-context perspective that the Innies have. We get so many moments where the things that are happening must be confusing for the Innie, but since the viewer gets to see the rest of that character's life (as an Outie) we can't really connect with how disjointing their experience must be. Removing the viewer's quasi-omniscience creates a whole new viewing experience. Since this is a full season's worth of footage, even with all the Outie aspects of the show removed, the length of the edit was too long for single viewing. I split everything into a pair of feature-length segments: Part One (1hr 53min) and Part Two (2hr 13min).
