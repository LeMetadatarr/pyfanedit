Subtitles Available:
Yes
Synopsis:
I didn’t hate Dark Fate, but I didn’t love it either. My biggest issue is that it ignores Terminator 3 and Salvation. It also ignores Genisys, but I hate Genisys, so I’m fine with that. I don’t hate T3 or Salvation. They’re not perfect, but I like them, especially in their edited forms. My goal with this project was to reconcile Dark Fate with T3 and Salvation and create a single, consistent continuity. Here is my new Terminator timeline: The Terminator – My preferred edit is Krausfadr’s. I actually enjoy Agent9’s a little more, but its opening conflicts with my T5. T2: Judgment Day – My edit, though most versions work well. Terminator 3: The Coming Storm – My edit. Terminator 4: Salvation – My edit. Terminator 5: Divergence – This project. No Genisys! (Except for the material I repurposed for T5.) So how does this work? Terminator 5: Divergence opens with Sarah’s recording in the hospital from Dark Fate (originally from T2), overlaid with footage of the nuclear explosions from both Genisys and T2. From there, we move directly to the beach scene from Dark Fate. Then we cut into the “Last Stand” Future War sequence from Genisys. Skynet is destroyed, but it manages to activate the time displacement weapon. Cut to Guatemala from Dark Fate. The Terminator that was sent back in time from Genisys kills John, creating a divergence in the timeline. New title card: TERMINATOR: DIVERGENCE — because the timeline has now split into something new. At this point, I introduce a text crawl, similar in style to The Terminator and Salvation, to explain what has changed and establish the new continuity. From there, it is Dark Fate, but with many additional trims and adjustments.
